# Preprocessing data sets article:
“Sex-stratified blood transcriptomic candidate biomarkers of multiple sclerosis (MS) identified by ensemble feature selection” - 

Repository-provided preprocessed expression data were used, with the original preprocessing procedures described by Nickles et al. [1]. 
The study investigated blood transcriptomic profiles in MS, including their association with interferon treatment (Betaseron, Avonex, and Rebif). 
The initially downloaded dataset (discovery and replication) comprised 690 expression profiles from people with MS and 125 profiles from healthy controls. 
Only profiles from untreated RRMS cases with available sex data were retained (222 profiles from untreated RRMS cases, and 86 healthy-control profiles). 
The expression matrix contained 18,725 probes. Repeated untreated expression profiles from the same participant across visits
were averaged to obtain one participant-level profile. 
The original discovery and replication subsets comprised distinct participants and were combined after participant-level aggregation. 
The final analytical dataset comprised 85 untreated MS cases (24 male and 61 female) and 60 healthy controls (19 male and 41 female).

[1] D. Nickles, H. Chen, M. Li, et al., Blood RNA profiling in a large cohort
of multiple sclerosis patients and healthy controls, Human Molecular
Genetics 22 (20) (2013) 4194–4205.
